# Google Play listing — English

**App name (max 30 chars):**
Celyah – Smart Robot

**Short description (max 80 chars):**
Chat with, see and control your Celyah robot from anywhere.

**Full description:**
Celyah is the companion app for the Celyah robot: a small robot with expressive eyes and real emotions that moves, talks and sees.

⚠️ Important: this app works with the Celyah robot (a DIY ESP32-S3 board). Without the robot the app does nothing.

✨ What you can do:
• 💬 Smart chat: talk to Celyah by voice or text powered by Gemini AI (using your own API key). She replies and changes her face to match her feelings.
• 🙂 Live robot face: watch Celyah's eyes and 45+ expressions animate on your phone.
• 📷 Live camera: see what the robot sees from anywhere (on a camera-equipped board).
• 🕹️ Full control: directions, speed, arm, ready-made gestures (wave, pick), manual or automatic mode.
• 📶 Easy setup: connect the robot to your home Wi-Fi over Bluetooth, no cables.
• 🌍 Remote access: control from anywhere in the world through an MQTT server you choose.
• 🔋 Robot status: energy, Wi-Fi, temperature and memory.
• 🌐 Arabic and English interface.

🔒 Privacy: all your settings and keys stay on your device. No ads, no tracking.

Permissions: Bluetooth (to connect to the robot), microphone (voice chat), internet (Gemini and remote control).
